Washout-thrust BTC bot: it sits in cash while the market grinds down and buys only the rare violent reversal — a sharp 15-minute washout (down 85 bps) followed by a strong recovery thrust (up 15 bps in 15 minutes). It exits on take-profit (+120 bps), stop-loss (-60 bps), a 30 bps trailing stop, or a stall rule that sells if the trade has not gained 15 bps within 8 bars. A late-window fallback softens the entry bars after bar 1200 so the bot still takes at least one trade. All decisions use integer basis-point math on bar closes, so runs are deterministic.
